Covers digital infrastructure, student and staff portal access, institutional email accounts, campus Wi-Fi connectivity, and Learning Management System (LMS) errors.

4 Example Complaints (2,840 Cases Logged):
  • · Student portal password reset & two-factor authentication lockout
  • · Campus Wi-Fi dead zones in lecture theaters and residence blocks
  • · Course registration portal timeout during semester enrollment
  • · Institutional email (@resolvehub.edu.ng) provisioning delay

2. Academic

Handles examination result discrepancies, missing continuous assessment scores, course credit unit conflicts, academic advising, and lecture timetable clashes.

4 Example Complaints (3,190 Cases Logged):
  • · Omitted or unuploaded semester examination grade on student transcript
  • · Continuous Assessment (CA) score calculation discrepancy
  • · Clashing examination venues or overlapping core course schedules
  • · Final-year project supervisor allocation and clearance sign-off delay

3. Finance / Bursary

Addresses tuition fee reconciliation, duplicate bank card deductions, unverified payment receipts, scholarship/bursary disbursements, and overpayment refunds.

4 Example Complaints (2,415 Cases Logged):
  • · Successful bank debit not reflecting on student bursary ledger
  • · Duplicate tuition or hostel fee deduction refund application
  • · Inability to generate stamped official bursary clearance receipt
  • · Delayed disbursement of approved work-study or scholarship stipend

4. Accommodation

Covers student residence halls, bed-space ballot allocations, hostel water supply and plumbing, room electrical sockets, sanitation, and common-room upkeep.

4 Example Complaints (1,980 Cases Logged):
  • · Water pump failure or broken plumbing pipes in residence hall wing
  • · Paid hostel bed-space allocation conflict or double assignment
  • · Damaged room door lock, window louver, or ceiling fan replacement
  • · Irregular waste disposal and washroom sanitation in hostel blocks

4 Example Complaints (1,120 Cases Logged):
  • · Unlit walkway or broken security floodlights along night study routes
  • · Report of stolen laptop or personal property inside reading rooms
  • · Unauthorized visitor entry or tailgating at residence hall gates
  • · Vehicle parking dispute or ID card confiscation inquiry at main gate

  • · Power outage or delayed backup generator switchover in Science Complex
  • · Broken seating desks and ventilation units in Lecture Hall 3

4 Example Complaints (1,040 Cases Logged):

  • · Correction of misspelled legal name or date of birth on registry file
  • · Smart ID card printing delay after biometric capture completion
