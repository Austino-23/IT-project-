Campus Wi-Fi Authentication Failure in Engineering Block B

Assigned to Engr. Tunde Bakare (Senior ICT Officer) · Department: ICT & Network Infrastructure Unit

Complaint Information & Evidence

Submitted statement and supporting files provided by the complainant

Since Monday morning (October 1, 2026), students and faculty inside Engineering Block B (2nd and 3rd floor lecture halls and CAD laboratories) have been unable to authenticate onto the ResolveHub-Campus-5G wireless network. Devices connect to the access point but fail at the 802.1X RADIUS login prompt with a "DHCP Lease / Authentication Timeout" error.

This is currently affecting over 180 final-year engineering students who require network access for cloud CAD simulations and continuous assessment portal submissions.
