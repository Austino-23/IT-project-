Structured Case Lifecycle

How ResolveHub Handles Your Complaint

Every submission follows a strict, auditable workflow from initial filing to complainant sign-off—preventing unacknowledged tickets or premature closures.

Explore Full 11-Step Workflow →
01. Submit & Reference

Lodge Complaint & Receive Case ID

Select the relevant category, describe the incident, and attach supporting evidence (receipts, photos, or documents). The system immediately generates a unique tracking ID.

Instant confirmation · Steps 1–3
02. Automated Routing

Department & Officer Assignment

Your complaint is automatically routed to the responsible department (ICT, Bursary, Facilities, Academic, etc.) and assigned to a dedicated Case Resolution Officer.

Under 2 hrs · Steps 4–5
03. Active Investigation

Transparent Updates & Dialogue

Follow timestamped investigation notes in real time. Communicate directly with the assigned officer and upload additional clarifications within the case thread.

24h–48h SLA · Steps 6–8
04. Verified Closure

User Confirmation & Feedback

No case is closed until you review the proposed resolution, confirm that your concern has been addressed, and submit a quality rating for institutional auditing.

Complainant verified · Steps 9–11
Platform Architecture

Built for Institutional Accountability

Traditional email inboxes and paper suggestion boxes lack tracking, ownership, and escalation rules. ResolveHub replaces ambiguity with measurable accountability.

01. Complete Auditability

Immutable Case Timeline & Status History

Every action—from initial filing and departmental assignment to staff remarks and evidence uploads—is recorded with an exact timestamp and officer identifier. Complainants and administrators see a clear, tamper-evident record of how every case progressed.

Timestamped Logs · Named Case Officers · Downloadable Case Summary
02. SLA Enforcement

Automated Escalation Matrix

Each category operates under defined Service Level Agreements (4h for critical safety/security, 24h–48h for standard cases). Cases approaching deadline automatically alert department heads and escalate to System Administrators.

Zero Stalled Tickets · Real-Time SLA Countdown
03. Protected Reporting

Role-Based Access & Privacy Controls

Sensitive complaints regarding security, harassment, or financial irregularities are restricted strictly to authorized investigators, protecting complainant identity against unauthorized viewing.

Strict Access Control · Confidential Handling
04. Evidence-Backed Resolution

Centralized Document & Media Evidence Vault

Attach payment receipts, portal screenshots, hostel maintenance photographs, or official correspondence directly to your case. Staff officers attach signed resolution memos or refund proofs before requesting closure confirmation.

PDF, JPG, PNG & DOCX Support · Complainant Sign-Off Required
