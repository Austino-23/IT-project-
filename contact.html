Every submitted complaint receives an automated Case ID immediately. Initial human triage occurs within 1 hour for Critical Security/Safety cases, within 4 hours for High Priority cases, and within 12–24 hours for Standard cases. Full resolution SLA targets range from 4 hours to 48 hours depending on category complexity.

You must review the resolution proof and confirm that the issue is resolved—or request reopening with additional remarks—before the ticket transitions to Closed.
ResolveHub tracks live SLA countdown timers on every open case. When 75% of the allotted time elapses without an official update, the department head receives an automated warning. If the SLA deadline is breached, the case status automatically changes to Escalated and is routed to the System Administrator and Ombudsman queue.
