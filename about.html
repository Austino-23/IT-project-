Platform Overview

What ResolveHub Is

ResolveHub is a centralized, web-based Digital Complaint Management and Case Resolution System built for academic and public institutions. Operating under the guiding mandate "Your Concern. Our Responsibility.", it serves as the single authoritative registry for receiving, routing, investigating, and resolving grievances across all institutional units.

Instead of scattering complaints across unmonitored email addresses, physical memo trays, or walk-in queues, ResolveHub assigns every issue a permanent tracking identifier, routes it to the appropriate department, and logs every interaction until verified closure.

Centralized Case Registry · 8 Operational Divisions · 100% Digital Audit Trail
Core Purpose

Why the System Exists

In traditional institutional environments, complainants frequently experience lost paperwork, unclear departmental jurisdiction, and weeks of silence without knowing who is handling their case. Simultaneously, administrators lack real-time visibility into recurring bottlenecks.

ResolveHub exists to eliminate administrative opacity. By enforcing strict Service Level Agreement (SLA) timers and requiring complainant confirmation before any ticket can be closed, the platform guarantees institutional responsiveness and data-backed service improvement.

Eliminates Lost Files · Enforces SLA Deadlines · Prevents Unverified Closures

Resolution Governance

How Complaints Are Handled

Every complaint is governed by objective Service Level Agreement (SLA) response windows, strict confidentiality controls, and multi-tier escalation safeguards.

Priority Tier Example Scenarios Initial Triage Target Resolution SLA Target Escalation Protocol
Critical / Urgent Campus security incident, total hostel water/power outage, exam portal lockout during tests Under 1 Hour 4 – 12 Hours Immediate alert to Department Head & Chief Security/ICT Officer
High Priority Duplicate tuition fee deduction, missing graduation clearance grade, faulty lab equipment Under 4 Hours 24 Hours Auto-escalates to Senior Resolution Lead if unupdated at 18 hours
Standard Priority Hostel furniture repair, transcript request status, general administrative inquiry Under 12 Hours 48 – 72 Hours Flagged in Admin Governance queue if SLA threshold is exceeded
End-to-End Lifecycle

Complete 11-Step Complaint Resolution Workflow

Follow the exact sequence every case travels through inside ResolveHub—from initial submission to final complainant confirmation and closure.

01

Submit Complaint

The user completes the structured submission form with incident details, location, urgency level, and supporting file attachments.

Actor: Complainant
↓
02

Case ID Generated

ResolveHub immediately assigns a unique, immutable tracking code (e.g., RH-2026-8492) and logs the submission timestamp.

Actor: System Automated
↓
03

Complaint Categorized

The complaint is classified under one of the 8 institutional categories (ICT, Academic, Finance/Bursary, Accommodation, Security, Facilities, Administration, or Other) with an SLA tier.

Actor: System / Triage
↓
04

Department Assigned

The categorized ticket is routed directly to the responsible operational department queue and triggers an SLA countdown timer.

Actor: Routing Engine
↓
05

Staff Assigned

A specialized Case Resolution Officer within that department is assigned ownership of the ticket and notified immediately.

Actor: Dept Lead / Admin
↓
06

Investigation

The assigned officer reviews attached evidence, inspects departmental records or physical facilities, and logs verified findings in the audit trail.

Actor: Case Staff
↓
07

Communication

Two-way progress updates and clarification requests are exchanged transparently between the officer and the complainant inside the case thread.

Actor: Staff & User
↓
08

Resolution

The officer implements the corrective action, uploads official resolution proof (e.g., adjusted receipt, repair photo, grade memo), and marks the case as Resolved.

Actor: Case Staff
↓
09

User Confirmation

The complainant reviews the staff resolution and either confirms that the issue is satisfactorily resolved or requests reopening if incomplete.

Actor: Complainant
↓
10

Rating & Feedback

Upon confirmation, the complainant submits a resolution quality rating (1–5 scale) and optional feedback to help audit departmental performance.

Actor: Complainant
↓
11

Case Closed

The case is formally archived as Closed with a complete, read-only audit log accessible to both the complainant and institutional administrators.
